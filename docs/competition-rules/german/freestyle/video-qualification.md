# German freestyle video qualification

Video qualification is a German national procedure, not a general IUF
substitute for attendance. Under the supplied national concept, it is limited
to Individual and Pair routines. It does not award a title or a place in the
current event's final; it is used only when allocating a place at the next
competition. Use the current season's form and confirm that the procedure has
not been amended. (German concept, §2.4, pp.8-9.)

## Eligibility and frequency

The concept lists documented injury, acute serious illness, or an exam /
professional obligation on the event day as qualifying reasons. Other
unavoidable circumstances need explanation and evidence. Vacation and social
events are not accepted. The start fee remains due. Generally, each rider may
receive only one approved video qualification in a qualification chain from
LM through the international event, with stated exceptions for closely
scheduled events and certain continuing injury cases at DM. (German concept,
§2.4, pp.8-9.)

## Video requirements

- Use the latest available competition video from an officially announced
  competition at Landesmeisterschaft level or higher.
- The video must be no more than two years old.
- For a Pair routine, the recorded and planned pair lineup must be identical.
- If two videos from the same competition exist, use the later/final-round
  video.
- A video routine can count in age-class place allocation but does not appear
  in the overall list. (German concept, §2.4, pp.9-10.)

## Application and evaluation

The separate form must be submitted per routine to both the freestyle
representatives and the event host, with supporting evidence and a video link,
at least **12 hours before** the event begins. The representatives decide
whether to accept the application; the concept says that decision cannot be
appealed. At the event, judges watch the video once on a laptop/tablet with
headphones, directly after the last live routine in that class/discipline;
pausing or replaying is not permitted. (German concept, §2.4, p.10.)

The supplied form, dated 9 June 2026, requests the competition, prior
qualification applications, discipline, age class, unavailable rider,
reason/evidence, latest official competition video details, and trainer /
rider signatures. The form also repeats the 12-hour submission requirement.
(Video qualification application, pp.1-2.)

## Late injury or illness at the event

If a serious injury or sudden illness at the venue makes continued
participation impossible, the jury lead and national representatives decide
the next step without delaying the event. An attestation must be provided
within three days afterward; otherwise the routine is disqualified and the
next routine moves up. (German concept, §2.4, p.9.)
