# German national freestyle event hosting

This note summarizes the supplied guidelines for hosting the 2027 South
German Championship (SDM), North German Championship (NDM), or German
Championship (DM). Use the requirements as a starting point for 2027 onward
and verify them against the current application call. The dates in the
supplied application process belong to the 2026 selection cycle.

## Facility

- Competition floor: at least **15 x 28 m**.
- At least six changing rooms, sufficient sanitary facilities, and a
  spectator stand with guideline capacity of at least 399 for SDM/NDM and
  499 for DM.
- A separate warm-up hall should be continuously available. If it is in
  another building, relay the event status through a screen or loudspeaker.
- If a visible hall center line differs from the competition-floor center,
  disclose this in the event announcement and mark the actual floor center.
(2027 hosting guidelines, §1, p.2.)

## Event operations

The guidelines call for event IT, a high-quality sound system, medals and
certificates, registration and age checks, start-fee accounting per start,
jury tables, schedule and awards operations, first aid, and video recording
of all routines with public availability. Food sales are expected; alcohol
sales are prohibited. The jury allocation and announcement must be approved
by the relevant representatives before publication. A coordination meeting
with the host, federations, and technical experts is planned about three
months before the event. (2027 hosting guidelines, §2, pp.2-3.)

## Event calendar and application dates

For the 2027 event cycle, the guidelines identify NDM/SDM from mid-March and
DM in May, with a Friday start. The stated application deadline (31 May 2026)
and award decision date (14 June 2026) have passed; use the dates in the
current call for future competitions. (2027 hosting guidelines, §§2-4,
pp.2-3.)
