# German national freestyle judging supplement

This page records German-specific scoring and jury procedures for 2027 onward.
Use it with the applicable German national season rules; it is not an IUF
judging rulebook. The supplied judges concept was updated September 2025 and
is marked effective from season 2026; check for any later replacement.
(Judges concept, pp.1, 11; German concept, §4, p.13.)

## National score calculation

The German concept specifies absolute points. Average each judge's score
within the three components, then calculate:

```text
overall = (presentation_average * 0.45 / 30
         + technique_average * 0.45 / 30
         + dismount_average * 0.10 / 10) * 100
```

Round the overall score to three decimal places. The highest and lowest
Presentation and Technique scores may be dropped only when there are at least
five judges in that component. Overall points determine the ranking. (German
concept, §4.1, p.13.)

## Club judge obligations

- Participating clubs provide trained judges; judges must be at least 15.
  The minimum is four judging assignments for every started block of two
  routines; the concept recommends adding a judge for each four routines.
- A preliminary-round assignment counts as two judging assignments. Missing
  assignments incur a stated 7 EUR fee per assignment; a short-notice
  withdrawal after the jury plan is published also incurs the fee if no
  replacement is supplied.
- The judge briefing is mandatory before the first assignment. Missing it
  incurs a stated 20 EUR fee, subject to the jury lead's exception for a
  promptly reported valid reason.
(Judges concept, §§1-2, pp.3-5.)

## Jury planning and conduct

- The jury lead and host are jointly responsible for schedule flow and a
  suitable quiet judging environment. The jury lead owns jury, scoring, and
  rule matters; the host owns event logistics.
- Keep the panel consistent across a preliminary group so routines can be
  compared for the overall list. Judges score independently. Avoid conflicts
  of interest and visible club affiliation.
- The jury plan should be published at least one week before the event; for
  SDM/NDM it must also be agreed with the national freestyle representatives
  before publication.
- Allow at least three minutes of scoring time per routine, avoid announcements
  during scoring, and keep scores and notes confidential.
(Judges concept, §§3-4, pp.5-8.)

The jury concept also defines training, licensing, and judge-register practices.
Those are German organizational procedures; consult the PDF before
administering training or handling register data. (Judges concept, §§5 and
"Jurorenregister und Datenschutz", pp.8-10.)
