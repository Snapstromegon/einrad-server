# German national freestyle format

This note summarizes German-specific format rules marked for the 2027 season
and later. The source is the shared concept dated 23 February 2026; verify
later amendments and event announcements before applying it. The concept's
IUF 2019 base statement is explicitly about the 2026 season and does not
establish the IUF base edition for 2027 onward. See the
[specificity chain](../../README.md).

## Disciplines and duration

The German national artistic events covered are female/male Individual,
Pairs, Small Group, and Large Group. From Landesmeisterschaft level, the
maximum routine lengths are:

| Event | Junior Expert | Expert |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Individual / Pair | 3 min | 3 min |
| Small Group | 5 min | 5 min |
| Large Group | 5 min | 5 min |

The concept says Standard Skill and X-Style are not held concurrently with
these other freestyle disciplines from Landesmeisterschaft level onward.
(German concept, §§1.1, p.1.)

## Season calendar

The concept places the national season in the first half of the year:
Landesmeisterschaften from mid-January, at least three weeks before SDM/NDM;
SDM/NDM from mid-March, at least three weeks before DM; and DM in May. Check
the current event calendar for later seasons. (German concept, §1.2, pp.1-2.)

## Age classes and finals

- The age reference date for all disciplines is 1 June. The oldest rider
  determines the age class for a pair or group. Use the season convention
  stated in the current concept when converting birthdays to a category.
  (German concept, §1.4, p.3.)
- Individual and pair age classes are grouped into three preliminary classes:
  Junior Expert (U11/U13/U15), Expert 1 (U17/U19/U21), and Expert 2
  (U23/U25/U30/U50/U70/70+). Finals are formed from the overall results within
  the relevant preliminary group: five Junior Expert places and four places
  from each Expert preliminary group (eight Expert final places total). Group
  events use Junior Expert and Expert; apply the 1 June age test to the
  oldest group rider. (German concept, §1.4, pp.3-4.)
- A preliminary group with fewer than five Expert or six Junior Expert
  routines goes directly to the final; age-class winners are still determined
  and recognized. (German concept, §1.5, p.5.)

## Advancement

The concept provides several routes to the next national competition:
top-three age-class places, age-class movers who place in the top three, and
qualifying overall-finalists who did not already receive a place through
their age class. For each group discipline, places 1-3 advance. Places must
be accepted within seven days; an unaccepted place may be offered to the next
eligible routine. A later replacement deadline applies, so confirm the
competition bulletin. (German concept, §§2.1-2.2, pp.6-7.)
