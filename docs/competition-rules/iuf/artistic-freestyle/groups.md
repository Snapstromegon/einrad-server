# Artistic freestyle: groups

## Group sizes

Under the IUF definition, a Small Group has **3-8 riders** and a Large Group
has **at least 9 riders**, with no maximum. A Large Group may include one
registered Head Coach. A rider may enter one Small Group and one Large Group
routine, but not two routines in the same group-size event, subject to the
Chief Judge's exception for a replacement due to illness, injury, or mishap.
(IUF 2026, §§6B.6.1-6B.6.1.2, p.109.)

## Age/category rules

IUF group age/category offerings differ from individual/pairs: Small Groups
use Junior Expert and Expert minimum categories; Large Groups have no minimum
age groups under the listed rule. (IUF 2026, §6E.5.3, p.130.)

## Group judging

The IUF uses the same Technical / Performance / Dismount components as other
artistic routines. The group is judged as a whole: difficulty should reflect
the average level across riders, the routine should use all riders effectively,
and group size alone does not increase difficulty. Teamwork, cooperation,
synchronization, use of the performing area, and the relative capabilities of
the riders matter. Group dismount scoring is adjusted for group size and uses
separate fall counting; the exact calculation is in §6D.4.0.1. (IUF 2026,
§§6D.1, 6D.2.1.2, 6D.2.2.1, 6D.2.3.2, 6D.3.1.2, 6D.3.2.2, 6D.4.0.1,
pp.120, 122-127.)
