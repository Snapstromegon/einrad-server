# Artistic freestyle: individual

## Scope

This page summarizes the IUF base rules. Use the IUF edition specified for the
competition. German national supplements are kept separately in
[German freestyle references](../../german/freestyle/README.md).

## Competition shape

- The individual routine is performed to music. Costumes, props, and any type
  and number of unicycles are allowed under the IUF rules; safety gear is not
  required. IUF time limit: **3 minutes maximum**. (IUF 2026, §§6A.1, 6B.1,
  6B.2, 6B.8.1, pp.107-111.)
- IUF individual categories are separated by men/women. Minimum categories
  depend on the event: IUF/continental championships use age groups plus
  Junior Expert and Expert; other competitions have a smaller minimum set.
  The event organizer specifies the offered categories. (IUF 2026,
  §6E.5-6E.5.1, pp.129-130.)

## Judging

IUF artistic scoring has Technical (45%), Performance (45%), and Dismounts
(10%). Technical considers successful unicycling skills and transitions,
mastery/execution, difficulty, and duration. Performance considers presence,
composition/choreography, and interpretation of music/timing. Unplanned
dismounts are counted separately; planned dismounts should be distinguished
from mistakes. (IUF 2026, §§6D.1-6D.4, pp.120-127.)

## Event-day rules to remember

- The performance clock and judging start at the start of the music or the
  rider's start signal, whichever comes first. The clock stops at the rider's
  endpoint or the limit. Work after the limit is not judged; riding past the
  limit reduces the score. (IUF 2026, §§6B.9.1-6B.9.2, pp.112-113.)
- Set-up and clean-up are each limited to two minutes under IUF rules; a messy
  or unsafe performance area can lead to disqualification. (IUF 2026,
  §§6B.9-6B.9.4, pp.112-113.)
- An IUF artistic protest is limited to calculation or other non-subjective
  errors, must be filed within 15 minutes of posting, and cannot challenge a
  judge's subjective score. (IUF 2026, §6B.4, p.108.)
