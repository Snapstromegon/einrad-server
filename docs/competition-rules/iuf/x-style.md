# X-Style

X-Style is a freestyle discipline focused on demonstrating difficult,
successfully completed unicycle skills. Unlike Artistic Freestyle, performance
quality and falls are not scored as separate components. (IUF 2026, §8A.1,
p.162; §8C.4, p.167.)

## Competition flow

- Split riders randomly into starting groups of at most ten.
- Default run durations are 1 minute in earlier rounds, 1 minute 30 seconds
  in semifinals, and 2 minutes in the final. The event director may announce
  another format; organizer rules allow runs between 1 and 2 minutes 30
  seconds.
- The top three riders in each group advance. If a tie affects the third
  advancing place, all tied riders advance. The final is one group, with
  places awarded from that round.
- If the event does not allow a final tie, tied riders take another one-minute
  run; repeat as needed to resolve it.
(IUF 2026, §§8B.5.3-8B.5.6.1, pp.164-165; §8D.3.1, p.168.)

## Judging

Judges rank riders in their starting group by comparing the difficulty of
their runs as a whole. Only executed skills count: the rider must reach a
controlled point. Higher quality (for example, clean or smooth execution)
can make the same skill more difficult. Falls and other negative aspects are
ignored. A rider cannot share a rank with another. The highest and lowest
placing points for each rider are discarded before the rest are summed; the
three lowest totals advance. (IUF 2026, §§8C.3-8C.5, pp.166-167.)

The judging table must have at least five judges and an odd number of judges
(or judge-pairs) so that each vote has equal weight. The IUF recommends that
each group be judged by two other starting groups. Riders may also judge
other groups. (IUF 2026, §8C.3, pp.166-167.)

## Venue and classes

For IUF / international events, the minimum performing area is 28 x 15 m; for
other events it is 12 x 9 m. If smaller than 28 x 15 m, dimensions must be
published at least one month ahead. IUF scoring uses Junior and Senior
classes, not age-group scoring. (IUF 2026, §§8D.1.1, 8D.5, pp.168-169.)
