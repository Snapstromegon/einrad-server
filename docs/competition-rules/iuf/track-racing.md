# Track racing

This quick reference covers IUF Chapter 2 (Track Racing), not Road Racing,
Mountain Unicycling, or Cyclocross. Those disciplines have separate rulebook
chapters and course rules.

## Events

IUF track events include 100 m, 200 m, 400 m, 800 m, 50 m One Foot, Wheel Walk,
4 x 100 m and 4 x 400 m relays, plus technical disciplines such as IUF Slalom.
The format, lane use, and remount allowance differ by event. (IUF 2026,
§§2B.2.1-2B.2.7, pp.32-34; §2B.4, p.39.)

- 100 m, 200 m, and 400 m are lane-bound; 200 m and 400 m use staggered
  starts. Remounting after a dismount is not allowed.
- 800 m allows remounting and may use either a stagger start with a breakline
  or a waterfall start.
- One Foot is a lane-bound 50 m event: both feet may pedal for the first 5 m,
  then the non-pedaling foot must be off the pedal.
- Wheel Walk is lane-bound, with 10 m for age groups capped at 10 and 30 m
  for other riders; riders propel the unicycle by pushing the tire, not the
  pedals/cranks.
- Relays allow remounts, require a baton, and have event-specific exchange
  zones and lane rules.

## Core race procedures

- No rolling starts. A false start occurs if a wheel moves forward before the
  start signal; the competition must announce which false-start option applies.
- In lane-bound racing, the tire may not touch lane markings. In non-lane-bound
  racing, passing is on the outside and riders must avoid obstruction.
- Where remounting is prohibited, a dismount means DNF. Where it is allowed,
  the rider must remount unassisted at the place the unicycle stopped.
- At the finish, the tire must cross the line while the rider is mounted and
  in control. For One Foot and Wheel Walk, control is defined by continuing
  the required riding style.
- Obstruction, lane infringement, and relay handover violations can lead to
  disqualification; protests and reruns follow the applicable referee rules.
(IUF 2026, §§2B.3.1-2B.3.3, pp.35-39.)

## Event configuration

The IUF rulebook details track dimensions, lanes, timing, officials, heats,
finals, and seeding separately. Use the exact discipline and competition
level's sections rather than assuming all track events use the same start,
remount, or final format. (IUF 2026, §§2B-2D, pp.25-53.)
