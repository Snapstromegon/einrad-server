# Juries on heats and finals

Jury categories are gone (#347, amends ADR 0013 on the deferred HEAT-5). Before, they were free names ("Einzel W – Expert 1 Vorlauf") with deployments and a day, unrelated to the heats and finals of #338, so the jury plan could not follow a skipped Vorlauf (ADR 0014) and every competition file listed them twice. Judges now register for, and the jury plan assigns them to, a **jury unit**: the Vorlauf of a heat, or a final.

Source: Jury-Konzept 15.09.2025 — "Die Jury bleibt jeweils für die Vorlaufklassen gleich, damit diese Küren alle miteinander verglichen werden können", and a Vorlauf counts as 2 Wertungseinsätze. A final is one run, fed by several heats, with one jury.

**One unit each, on the heat and on the final.** The Vorlauf unit lives on the heat, the final unit on the final; there is no separate jury-unit table. Both carry `jury_registrable`, `jury_deployments` (default 2 for a heat, 1 for a final) and the needed technical, performance and dismount judges (5, 5, 3), built into the binary. A registration or assignment references exactly one of them (`heat_id` or `final_id`, a CHECK enforces one).

**UUIDs.** Heats and finals get a UUID (`id`), which registrations and assignments reference, so renaming either keeps the jury. Every other reference to a heat or a final is by id too (`categories.heat_id`, `final_slots.final_id` and `heat_id`, `timeplan.final_id`, `timeplan_slots.heat_id`), so a rename is one update of the `name` column; the admin API addresses heats and finals by id and returns the name as a label. The competition file keeps the names: the import resolves them to ids, the export writes names (no ids in files). A replace from a competition file recreates heats and finals, so their ids change on every import (an admin tab opened before it gets 404s for its heats and finals until it is reloaded), and it re-links registrations by kind and name and puts the others on the remap file as before (`jury_unit = "heat: E EW"` or `"final: Finale EW"`). Deleting a heat or final is refused (409) while judges are registered or assigned to its unit.

**Registrable.** A unit takes registrations when `jury_registrable` and, for a heat, not skipped. Group heats are skipped by default, so only their final is offered. A skipped Vorlauf and a switched-off unit never take a new registration, on any path (club, jury lead moving or resolving a registration); a judge's existing registration of that task stays when the club edits the judge, but no other task can be added there.

**A heat skipped after judges registered keeps counting.** The club registered in good faith and pays nothing for the organizer's decision, so the credited deployments stay. The same holds for a unit the jury lead switched off by hand, and for a final: any registration on a unit that no longer takes registrations is flagged and keeps counting. The UI says "Vorlauf entfällt" only for a skipped Vorlauf, otherwise "Anmeldung geschlossen". The registration is flagged, derived from `skip` and never stored (un-skipping clears it), and the jury lead moves it with a dropdown in the admin judges view; a registration the judge already has at the target merges.

**The day is derived** from the timeplan: a heat's from its categories' first block, a final's from its block, `null` while it is not planned. The order of units follows the timeplan as well (first block, units not in it last). Neither is stored any more.

**Competition file.** `[[jury_categories]]` is rejected with a message and a short example, like the slot labels of ADR 0013; the jury fields go on `[[heats]]` and `[[finals]]` (`jury_registrable`, `jury_deployments`, `jury_needed_technical`, `jury_needed_performance`, `jury_needed_dismounts`) and the export writes the ones that differ from the defaults.

**Migration.** A jury category maps to a unit only where the match is unambiguous: its exact name is a heat's or a final's, or its name without " Vorlauf" is a heat's, and not more than one candidate fits. Its deployments and needed judges move to the unit; its day is dropped. Registrations of other categories go on a remap list in the database (`judge_registration_unmapped`), shown in the admin judges view for the jury lead to move to a unit or drop; they **count for nothing until then** (decided: a registration the migration could not map is not credited, so the club sees a gap rather than a guess). Assignments of unmapped categories are dropped, the jury lead plans those again. There is no fuzzy matching: a wrong guess would show only on the event day.
