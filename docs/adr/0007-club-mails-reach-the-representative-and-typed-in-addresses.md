# Club mails reach the club representative and typed-in addresses too

A club mail (Rundmail, #257) goes to each selected club's club contact and, in the same mail, its club representative when the club named someone else; the organizer team can also type in addresses, each getting a mail of its own, with the data of a club they assign the address to (without one, the mail can't use club data). This goes beyond ADR 0002's note that the Ausschreibung's Datenschutz section covers email only for the club contact: the club representative's address was collected for objections, and typed-in addresses aren't in the tool at all. We chose it over writing to the club contact only because changes to fees, deadlines or the jury rules concern both people responsible for a club, and a club's treasurer or a press contact often isn't either of them.

## Consequences

- The Ausschreibung's Datenschutz section should say that the club representative's address is used for mails about the competition.
- A typed-in address assigned to a club receives that club's data, e.g. its open amount; whoever sends is responsible for the address being the club's.
- Every club mail is kept with each mail as sent, and everyone allowed to send club mails (Admin, Juryleitung, Kasse) can read all of them, including amounts a jury lead can't use in a mail of their own.
